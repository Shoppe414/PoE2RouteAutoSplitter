PoE2 Route AutoSplitter v2.0.2 - Validation Summary

Riverbank correction
- Campaign 100% ordered exploration route: 99 route rows / 99 LiveSplit segments.
- Campaign Any% ordered exploration route: 78 route rows / 78 LiveSplit segments.
- Both ordered campaign routes begin with G1_1 | The Riverbank.
- Ordered campaign ASL uses successor-entry completion when the first route row is G1_1.
- The active segment completes only when the next configured route area is entered; arbitrary departures are not completion events.
- Final Cuachic Vault + Ziggurat Refuge handling remains the dedicated two-stage Ziggurat entry sequence.

Flexible/checklist behavior
- Riverbank is included in the Act 1 / Campaign All Areas / Campaign Any% checklist data.
- When G1_1 is also @start, its visit is counted implicitly at timer start without a zero-time LiveSplit split.
- Existing timed checklist .lss row counts therefore remain valid.

Merged Exploration + Boss Rush
- Campaign 100%: 99 exploration + 67 bosses = 166 objectives / LiveSplit rows.
- Campaign Any% v0.5: 78 exploration + 40 required bosses = 118 objectives / LiveSplit rows.
- Premade mixed campaign routes opt into @areaCompletion=successor.
- Mixed area objectives are armed on entry and complete only on their configured successor entry.
- A queued-area mechanism supports the final Cuachic Vault + Ziggurat Refuge objectives on the same transition.
- Custom mixed routes remain entry-based unless @areaCompletion=successor is explicitly added.

Campaign Boss Rush auto-start
- Modes 06, 07, 08, and 09 contain an enabled-by-default autoStart setting.
- Those four ASLs seek Path of Exile 2 logs/Client.txt to EOF on attach and watch only newly appended lines.
- G1_1 / The Riverbank is recognized from the internal Generating level line or the `You have entered The Riverbank.` fallback.
- Riverbank start detection is entry-based only; no departure-based logic was added.
- Boss events continue to come exclusively from poe2_boss_events.log.
- Modes 10 and 11 (Pinnacle) retain manual start and contain no Riverbank auto-start setting.
